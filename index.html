You're ready to buy a home when you have stable income, a good credit score (typically 620+), a down payment saved (usually 10-20% of the home price), and you plan to stay in the area for at least 3-5 years. Our team can help assess your financial readiness and connect you with mortgage pre-approval services.

Spring and early summer (March through June) are traditionally the best times to sell, as buyers are more active and homes show better with good weather.

Your offer should be based on comparable sales in the area, the home's condition, current market conditions, and your budget.

Closing costs typically range from 2-5% of the home's purchase price and include fees for loan origination, appraisal, title insurance, attorney fees, and taxes.
